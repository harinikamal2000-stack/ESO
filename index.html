2 · SWOT

Our SWOT on one page

Strengths

  • One tool for SEO, accessibility, and AI search visibility
  • Fixes you review and approve before they go live
  • Free audit in about a minute, plus a 14-day trial with no card

Weaknesses

  • Few public reviews and case studies
  • Visible website errors that look unfinished (section 3)
  • Pricing and platform details are not the same everywhere

Opportunities

  • More businesses need help showing up in AI search
  • Accessibility rules are getting stricter in the EU and US public sector
  • Agencies want tools they can offer under their own brand

Threats

  • Big suites are adding AI visibility features
  • Cheap single-purpose tools compete on price
  • One bad automatic fix could damage trust

5 · Scope

What is in this plan, and what is not

In this plan

  • Fix the 8 website errors in section 3
  • Set up tracking for free audits, trials, and demo requests
  • LinkedIn and Instagram upgrade: cadence and content
  • One small paid test, after budget approval
  • One demo script, one trial checklist, and follow-up messages
  • A weekly written update and a monthly competitor check
  • A final report and a proposal for the next phase

Explicitly not in this plan (later phase)

  • New product features or new AI modules
  • Full website redesign beyond the fixes and content pages
  • Price changes or new plans
  • Paid ads at scale, beyond the test
  • X and Facebook growth campaigns
  • New integrations or API expansion
  • India-specific accessibility product (GIGW, IS 17802)
  • Languages other than English
  • Events, webinars, or podcasts
  • Team hiring or role changes

Anything in the second list is a later phase. We confirm its time and cost in writing before we start it.
